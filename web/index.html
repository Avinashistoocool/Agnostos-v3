Questions about Agnostos

Agnostos is a free, open-source research internship outreach tool. It reads your resume, finds professors whose recent papers overlap with your work, drafts one honest email per professor, sends them slowly from your own inbox, and helps you answer replies.

Yes. You connect your university or Gmail account with an app password. Mail goes out from your address, and replies land in your normal inbox.

No. Drafts are told to describe what you built and tried, never results you don't have. Anything it can't know is left as [FILL] for you.

Forty by default, five to ten minutes apart, only between 9 and 5 in each professor's own time zone, on weekdays. You can lower it.

The app is free. Drafting uses your own model key (Groq is free; OpenAI gpt-5-mini costs about half a cent per email).

Always. Send review copies to yourself, export .eml files, or approve replies one by one before anything leaves.

Credentials are encrypted at rest on the server and are only handed to your own sending job. You can delete them at any time.
